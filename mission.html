Our mission

Make medical device research more open and accessible.

Openwater brings researchers, developers, and clinicians together around shared hardware designs, software, documentation, and research methods.

Why we build in the open

When tools and methods can be inspected, adapted, and tested by others, more teams can contribute to reproducible medical device research.

Access

Publish designs and code so research teams can study the platform and build on it.

Reproducibility

Document protocols, measurements, and software versions so results can be checked and repeated.

Collaboration

Welcome contributions to engineering, documentation, quality, and research methods.

What the community is working on

Open-LIFU

An open-source focused ultrasound platform for neuromodulation research and development.

Open-Motion

An open-source optical platform for research into blood flow and blood volume measurement.

Research use only. Open-LIFU and Open-Motion are not cleared or approved by the FDA for clinical diagnostic or therapeutic use. Human-subjects research requires appropriate institutional and regulatory authorization.

Take part

You can contribute without a device: improve code, review documentation, strengthen testing, or help make research methods clearer.
