A Better Question

Practical evaluation questions

You would not prescribe a dose without knowing where it goes. Why should acoustic energy be different? When evaluating any acoustic modality, ask:

  • Where does the energy go? What is the measured field shape, and at what depth does it concentrate?
  • How is it measured? What bench characterization exists, and can you inspect the methods?
  • What is claimed — and what isn't? Which statements are backed by evidence, and which are adjectives?
