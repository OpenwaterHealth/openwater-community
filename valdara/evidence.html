Methods & Limitations

How we measure — and where measurement ends

How are the acoustic field maps produced?

Field maps are acquired with calibrated hydrophone scanning in standardized test media under controlled bench conditions, following recognized acoustic characterization practices. Maps represent pressure distributions of specific system configurations.

Do bench measurements predict performance in tissue?

Not directly. Human tissue is heterogeneous; attenuation, aberration, and interfaces alter the field. Bench data describes the engineered field shape — it does not demonstrate biological effect or clinical performance.

Why publish limits alongside measurements?

Because credibility is a design requirement. A field map without its limitations is marketing; with them, it is evidence. Every asset in this room states what it shows and what it does not.

When will clinical evidence be available?

We do not publish predicted regulatory or clinical timelines. Milestones are posted to the In Development page as they are completed — not before.
