Regulatory Pathway

Discipline is the brand discipline

Our external language stays descriptive and evidence-gated until clearance. Here is what that means in practice.

▣

What we say now

Mechanism-descriptive language only: depth-targeted acoustic therapy, focused by design, field shape, and bench measurements.

⊘

What we don't say

No safety, efficacy, superiority, pricing, ROI, waitlist, or clearance-timing language until it is unlocked by evidence and regulatory status.

✓

What changes later

Post-clearance language escalates only within cleared indications and substantiation — reviewed against our claims matrix.
