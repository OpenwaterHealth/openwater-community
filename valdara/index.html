Energy Has a Shape

Where does the energy
actually go?

Not all acoustic energy behaves the same. Some devices spread energy broadly along the path. Valdara is designed to converge energy to a defined focal zone at a controlled depth — so the field itself, not adjectives, tells the story.

How It Works

From wave to focal zone

01 Generate

The handpiece generates acoustic waves using a precisely controlled transducer array.

02 Converge

Waves are shaped and timed to converge inside the body, reinforcing at the target depth.

03 Target

Energy is concentrated in a compact focal zone while intervening tissues receive significantly less.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers

What is depth-targeted acoustic therapy?

It is an investigational approach that uses focused acoustic energy to reach a specific depth within the body while minimizing exposure along the path. The mechanism — a defined focal zone at a controlled depth — is what distinguishes it from broadly distributed energy modalities.

Is Valdara available for clinical use?

No. Valdara is a medical device in development and has not received FDA clearance or approval. It is not available for commercial sale in the United States.

What has Valdara demonstrated so far?

Our work to date is bench characterization: acoustic field mapping, focal geometry measurement, and system verification. We have not demonstrated clinical outcomes or benefits — see the Evidence Room for exactly what we have and have not shown.
