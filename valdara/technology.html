How It Works

Generate. Converge. Target.

What this does — and does not — show

Glossary

Four words worth remembering

Focus

The convergence of acoustic waves toward a single region, where wavefronts reinforce one another to concentrate energy.

Depth

The controlled distance beneath the surface at which the focal zone forms — selectable to suit different anatomical targets.

Field

The full three-dimensional distribution of acoustic energy in tissue — the thing we map, measure, and show.

Dose

The amount of acoustic energy delivered to a region over time. Knowing where energy goes is the precondition to knowing the dose.

When you leave this page, remember the visual difference — even if you forget the vocabulary.
